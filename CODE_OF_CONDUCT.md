# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This project is about reading political posts carefully, which means contributors will
disagree about politics. Disagree about the claim and the evidence, never about the person.

## Our standard

Things that make this project work:

- Assuming the other person is arguing in good faith.
- Criticising an analysis, a prompt or a line of code, rather than whoever wrote it.
- Accepting that a verdict you dislike may still be correctly derived, and saying so.
- Taking correction without defensiveness, and giving it without contempt.

Things that will get a contribution closed and, on repetition, a contributor blocked:

- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained hostility toward a contributor.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private, including unwanted contact.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Using the project to campaign against a person, a party or a group, rather than to check claims.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.

## On political content

Unfold analyses posts by real politicians and public figures. Sample posts, fixtures and eval sets
will contain claims that people find objectionable. Including a post as test data is not an
endorsement of it, and asking for a post to be analysed is not an attack on its author.

Contributions that select posts to embarrass one side rather than to test the analyser are out of
scope and will be closed. The benchmark set aims for a spread across the political spectrum on
purpose.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **ettore@amphoralogistics.com**. Reports are handled privately. The maintainers
will look at what happened and respond within a week, with anything from a private word to a
permanent block from the repository.

Maintainers who do not hold to this document are subject to it the same as anyone else.
